## The short version

Itminan has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It
collects nothing about you. Your data stays on your device, except for the
coordinates needed to answer a query.

## What the app accesses

**Location.** With your permission, Itminan reads your device's location to
calculate prayer times, the Qibla direction, and to find nearby mosques. You
can decline and instead choose a city by hand; the app remains fully usable.

**Device compass.** The Qibla screen reads the magnetometer to show your
heading. This never leaves the device.

## What is sent off the device, and to whom

Only what is required to answer a request. There is no account, so nothing sent
is linked to an identity.

| Sent | Recipient | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Latitude and longitude | `api.aladhan.com` | Calculate prayer times and the Hijri date |
| Latitude, longitude, radius | Overpass API (OpenStreetMap) | Find nearby mosques |
| A city name you type | Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) | Look up coordinates for a city |
| Surah number | `api.alquran.cloud`, `cdn.islamic.network` | Fetch Qur'an text and recitation |

These are independent third-party services with their own privacy practices.
Itminan sends them no identifier, no device ID, and no advertising ID.

## What stays on your device

Settings, bookmarks, reading position, tasbih counts, Zakat figures, cached
prayer timetables, and cached Qur'an text are all stored locally using the
device's own storage. None of it is transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app
deletes all of it.

**Your Zakat figures never leave your device.**

## Notifications

If you enable prayer reminders, they are scheduled locally by your device.
There is no push server and no message is sent over the network.

## Children

Itminan collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Your rights

Because the app holds no data about you on any server, there is nothing to
request, export, or erase remotely. To erase everything the app has stored,
either use *Settings → Clear cached data* / *Reset all settings*, or uninstall.
